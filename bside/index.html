About BSides Lafia

Security BSides is a global framework of community-organized, independent cybersecurity events created to expand the conversation for Information Security professionals. BSides events offer an intimate, open environment for attendees to exchange ideas, present technical research, and engage in hands-on learning.

BSides Lafia brings this global movement to Nasarawa State, Nigeria. Our mission is to foster local talent, unite security researchers, developers, students, and industry professionals, and create an inclusive space for technical knowledge sharing, mentorship, and collaboration.

The CTF Arena (Capture The Flag)

Put your offensive and defensive security skills to the test in our live competitive Capture The Flag (CTF) challenge. Whether you're a seasoned pentester or an aspiring security student, tackle real-world puzzles spanning web exploitation, cryptography, reverse engineering, and forensics.

Join our active community on Discord to find teammates, prep for practice labs, and get real-time announcements regarding scoreboard rules and bounties.
